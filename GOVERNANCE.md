# Governance

## Roles

- **Contributor** - anyone with a merged PR.
- **Reviewer** - trusted with review authority in a defined area.
- **Maintainer** - merge rights, release authority, listed in MAINTAINERS.md.
- **Security Maintainer** - handles private disclosures per SECURITY.md.

## Becoming a maintainer

Sustained, high-quality contribution over at least three months, nominated by
an existing maintainer, approved by lazy consensus of maintainers over five
working days.

## Decision making

Lazy consensus. Any maintainer may request discussion; unresolved matters go to
a simple majority vote of maintainers.

## Areas

Each maintainer owns at least one area end to end: control plane, data plane,
identity/WSO2, spec, formal models, benchmarks, docs, release.
